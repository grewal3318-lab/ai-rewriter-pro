AI Rewriter Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

AI Rewriter Pro is a browser extension that rewrites selected text using AI models. This policy explains what data is and is not collected.

Data We Do Not Collect

AI Rewriter Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • We do not collect your browsing history or the content of pages you visit
  • We do not collect the text you select or rewrite
  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies

API Keys

Your API keys for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and OpenRouter are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never transmitted to us or any third party. They are sent directly from your browser to the respective AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or OpenRouter) when you use the extension.

Your Prompts

Your 15 custom prompts are stored locally in your browser on your own device only. They are never sent to us or any server other than the AI provider you choose when activating a rewrite.

Third Party AI Providers

When you perform a rewrite, the selected text and your prompt are sent directly to your chosen AI provider. Each provider has their own privacy policy: Anthropic (anthropic.com), OpenAI (openai.com), Google (ai.google.dev), OpenRouter (openrouter.ai).
